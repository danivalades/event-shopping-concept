Round 3

The tinted blocks in the product tiles went, because they distract. Each tile now carries a small icon that only says a product goes here: a dress, shoes, a bag or, for accessories, a pair of earrings. Every frame is the chosen S2-A page. Three axes. Icon style: I1 is a thin outline, I2 a solid silhouette in soft gray, I3 a heavier outline of simple shapes. Size: Z1 is about 28px, Z2 about 40px. Tone: T1 is neutral gray, matching the plus under each tile; T2 is the product’s own tint, reduced, so the color filters still read. The panel shows all four icons in each style at large size. S3-F was chosen. S3-H is S3-F with the first section ordered the way the app deals it, mostly dresses with a shoe or bag now and then.
